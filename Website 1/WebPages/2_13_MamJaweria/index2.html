Islamabad

Islamabad is the capital city of Pakistan. It is known for its greenery, peaceful environment, and modern infrastructure.

Lahore

Lahore is the cultural capital of Pakistan. It is famous for historical places, food, and festivals.

Karachi

Karachi is the largest city of Pakistan. It is the economic hub and has major ports and industries.

Quetta

Quetta is the capital of Balochistan. It is known for its mountains and cold climate.

Peshawar

Peshawar is one of the oldest cities in South Asia. It is famous for its history and traditional markets.
